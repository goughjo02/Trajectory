# Represent experience accurately

Use the person's evidence, not an assumed seniority or profession.

- Demonstrated experience: describe what they actually did, at what scope, and with what support.
- Working knowledge: practical familiarity, without implying specialist depth.
- Exposure: observed, assisted, studied, or briefly tried; say which.
- Interest: a future direction, not an existing capability.

Separate their contribution from team achievements. Preserve approximate dates and estimates. Do not turn qualitative outcomes into invented metrics, intentions into results, or familiarity into certification. Retain disclosure limits and distinguish public-safe evidence from restricted detail.
